// security

On-chain authorities & security architecture

Total supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 $AGNT and cannot be increased. Mint authority is revoked at launch in the same sitting as the initial mint — once the 1B exists, no further $AGNT can ever be created. The bot does not need mint authority because it operates on a transfer model: vault transfers AGNT to operational, operational transfers to contributors. Every transfer is auditable against the published curve formula.
